Tally marks scratched by hand — for counts a person could actually keep: seats left, rooms this month, intros made.

```jsx
<TallyCount count={12} color="violet" height={36} label="12 rooms this month" />
```

Marks tilt slightly and every fifth is slashed. Good up to ~30; past that use `MarkerBar` for the proportion instead. Never mix tally and bar in the same row.
