Die-cut sticker for slapping a label or doodle icon onto a card, photo, or headline.

```jsx
<Sticker tone="ink">new</Sticker>
<Sticker tone="yellow" round size={46}><img src="assets/icons/zap.svg" width="22" alt=""/></Sticker>
```

The white keyline is what makes it read as a sticker — keep it. Two or three per view maximum, always overlapping the edge of something.
