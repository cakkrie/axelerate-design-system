Speech bubble for testimonials, member quotes, and playful asides.

```jsx
<Bubble tone="sky" tail="bl" who="Mara · seed founder" tilt={-1}>Two intros, one week. Both closed.</Bubble>
```

Tails: `bl`/`br`/`tl`/`tr` point back at whoever is speaking — a bubble always has one. Mix 2–3 tones in a cluster; alternate tail sides so a group reads as a conversation, not a list.
