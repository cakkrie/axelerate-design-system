Sticky note for asides, reminders, and "one more thing" moments — never for primary content.

```jsx
<StickyNote tint="butter" tape heading="bring a friend" label="house rule" tilt={-3}>Members who host get first pick of seats.</StickyNote>
```

One or two per view, tucked into whitespace or overlapping a card corner. `heading` renders in Lacquer; body text stays Instrument Sans.
