Manila-folder card for service menus, capability lists, and "what you get" sections — the playful, colourful counterpart to `Card`.

```jsx
<FileCard tint="sky" tab="01" title="Curated rooms" items={['Eight seats','One topic','Zero lurkers']} tilt={-2} />
```

Fan 3–5 side by side with alternating tilts (−3…+3) and overlapping margins for the stacked-folder look. Tints: violet, sky, coral, orange, butter, lilac, paper. Pass `children` instead of `items` for custom content.
